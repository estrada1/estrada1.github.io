Three-quarter, project-based graduate course on prototyping embedded systems. Each quarter concluded with intensive final projects completed in teams of four, including an interactive Batman-themed arcade game with IR sensors and accelerometers, an autonomous battleship competition robot, and a wirelessly controlled rover using event-driven software framework with SPI communication.
