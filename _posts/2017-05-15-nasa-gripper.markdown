Collaboration between the Biomimetics and Dexterous Manipulation Lab and Autonomous Systems Lab on a NASA Early Stage Innovations grant. Adapted a curved surface gecko adhesive gripper for use on a free flyer table. Modeled force constraints and dynamics while grasping translating, spinning objects and experimentally verified grasp boundaries.
