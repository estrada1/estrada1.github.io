Designed and tested gecko-inspired adhesive gripper on 2D free flyer testbed. Collaboration between the Biomimetics and Dexterous Manipulation Lab and [Autonomous Systems Lab](http://asl.stanford.edu/) on a NASA Early Stage Innovations grant to equip [Assistive Free Flyers](http://ssl.mit.edu/spheres/) (AFFs) on the International Space Station with gecko-gripper appendages.

Wrist compliance allowed for grasping with misalignment and relative velocity. Modeled planar dynamics and successful grasping conditions on a 2D testbed.
