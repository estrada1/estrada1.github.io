Designed and manufactured the structure of a soft force sensor for senior thesis at MIT. Used 3D printed molds to cast woven fiberglass cloth into polyurethane film encasing softer silicone. Hall effect sensors mounted above magnets on the deformable pad correlated deflection to force for use in the MIT Cheetah paw pad.
