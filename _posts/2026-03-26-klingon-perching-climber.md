title: "KlingOn: a perching climbing prototype"
---

<img src="/assets/img/klingon_glamour.jpg" alt="KlingOn prototype" width="400"/>

A compact multimodal robot prototype engineered to be thrown at vertical glass surfaces, adhere on contact, and subsequently climb upward. The development presented a notable mechanical challenge: coordinating climbing locomotion using only a single actuator, ultimately requiring integration of linkages, latches, and cams to achieve the functionality.
