Worked as part of student team of five, advised by a surgeon, to develop the SutureTie, a hand-held device using zip-tie sutures to occlude blood flow through discrete sections of the liver. Produced proof-of-concept prototype demonstrated on ex-vivo porcine liver. Project involved defining functional requirements, reviewing prior art, and observing a liver resection surgery.
