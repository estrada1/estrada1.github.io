Maintainer and developer of the k-rock 2 sprawling-gait quadruped robot with 21 Dynamixel motors. Implemented a surface swimming gait via an undulating tail and spine. Used simulation and laboratory experiments to predict traversability. Engineering work involved layering ROS onto the C++ controller architecture and revamping compilation with CMake.
