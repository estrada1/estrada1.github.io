Enabled MAVs to perch and rest on vertical surfaces. Designed gecko adhesive grippers that stick to smooth, flat surfaces and collaborated with controls group at UPenn to demonstrate perching under indoor motion capture. Featured in The New York Times: "What You Get When You Blend a Drone and a Gecko".
